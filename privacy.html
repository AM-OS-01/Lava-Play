Privacy Policy

Effective September 19, 2026

Information collected by Fun Lava

Fun Lava does not require an account and does not collect, store, or sell personal data. Fun Lava does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Microphone access

Fun Lava may request microphone access when Sound Reactive Lava is enabled. Microphone audio is processed locally on your device so the lava can respond to nearby sound.

Audio is not recorded, stored, or sent to a server by Fun Lava.

In-app purchases

Fun Lava may offer in-app purchases through Apple. Payment processing and purchase history are handled by Apple and are subject to Apple's terms and privacy practices.

Website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Fun Lava does not add analytics or tracking cookies to this website. GitHub may process information as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Contact

For privacy questions, email skymaze.support@gmail.com. Information you send by email is used only as needed to respond to your request or meet legal obligations.

Changes

This policy may be updated as Fun Lava changes. The effective date above will be updated when changes are made.
